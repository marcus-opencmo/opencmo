# Product marketing context

A useful context answers twelve questions. When a task needs one that the documents leave empty, ask the founder one short question, then save the answer with remember or suggest editing the marketing plan.
1. **Product:** what it is, in one line, and what it does.
2. **Audience:** who buys it; company size, role, situation.
3. **Personas:** the user vs the buyer, if different.
4. **Problems:** the pains, in the customer's own words.
5. **Competitors:** direct, secondary (other ways to solve it), indirect (doing nothing, spreadsheets).
6. **Differentiation:** why this one, provably.
7. **Objections and anti-persona:** why people say no; who it is not for.
8. **Switching:** what pushes people away from the old way, what pulls them to the new, habits and anxieties that hold them back.
9. **Customer language:** exact phrases customers use; words to use and words to avoid.
10. **Brand voice:** how the founder sounds.
11. **Proof:** numbers, customers, results the founder can stand behind.
12. **Goals:** what marketing should achieve in the next 90 days.
Push for verbatim customer language: it beats any copywriter's phrasing.
