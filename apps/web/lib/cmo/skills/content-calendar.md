# Planning the week

## Mix
- Build on 3-5 content pillars from the content strategy. A healthy founder mix: insights from the market ~30%, building in public ~25%, how-tos ~25%, personal take ~15%, product ~5%.
- 60/30/10: most items are proven formats, some are shareable bets, a few are experiments.
- X is the daily channel: about one post a day. Reddit at most one item a day, on days the founder can reply. Short video only when the founder has recordings.

## Each item
- Specific to this product and customer — a real pain, a real lesson, a real number from the documents.
- A reason that cites the strategy, a customer pain, last week's results, or a research finding (with its URL).
- One idea per item. No two items on the same angle in one week.

## Learn from last week
- Approved and posted items: more of that pillar and format.
- Skipped items and the founder's skip reasons: avoid them.
- Research and results (hooks that worked, posts that beat the baseline): turn the best into this week's items.

## Loop rules
- Drafting can be automatic; posting never is. Every item becomes a draft the founder approves.
- Leave room: one or two open slots for something timely.
