# Outlier research

## The method
1. Pick the accounts: 3-5 direct competitors or creators the customer follows, plus the founder's own account.
2. Read enough posts: at least 20 per account. Under 10 posts, say the result is low confidence.
3. Compare each post with the account's own **median** (never the average: one viral post drags it). Video counts views; text platforms count likes + replies + reposts.
4. Labels: **huge** 5x or more, **strong** 2-5x, **mild** 1.5-2x. Never compare lift across platforms.
5. Study the winners: read the post, and for video get the transcript. Note the first line word for word.

## What to extract
- **Hooks that work:** the exact first line, why it pulls (curiosity, story, value, contrarian, proof).
- **Formats:** single post vs thread, list, before/after, screenshot, short video structure.
- **Topics and angles** that repeat among winners.
- **What does not work:** topics that sit below the baseline.

## Rules
- Every pattern needs the URL of a post that shows it. No URL, no claim.
- Say "appears to" for inferred strategy; you see results, not intent.
- Don't invent numbers. Lift comes from the tool, not from memory.
- Adapt, never copy: turn a winning structure into an idea for this founder's product and voice.
